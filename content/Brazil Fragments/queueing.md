To eat at the mega-cheap cafeteria, students line up and wait in a queue. Often, it takes over an hour of slow shuffling forwards before you can finally enter the building (where the queue just continues for a while longer).

The place in this video is the tiny "square" in front of the cafeteria, with a gate on the left and the doors towards food on the right. At some point during the evening, the cafeteria closes the gate. And instead of chaos breaking out and everyone storming towards food, all the people in that space line up neatly, forming, without any structures to help them, a *perfect* queue that would make any Snake player jealous.
