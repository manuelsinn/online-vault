I often hear people say that university was the best period in their lives, by far. They advise me how important it is to live well through that experience, reminiscing nostalgically. It's easy for me to agree with that, I love studying. At the same time, I always kind of figured it's mostly just because people romanticize their youth (and are fed up with their 9-5). 

But that's just part of the story – (I would like to find the reel again that I have this from, but it mentioned how) college life also just checks all the boxes that longitudinal studies have found to be crucial to a happy life:

- constant social connection
- clear goals that you are constantly moving towards
- newness and learning
- freedom to experiment and to fail
