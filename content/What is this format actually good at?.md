What sort of information, story, idea can you get across uniquely through [[Digital Gardens|networked writing like this]]? What can interlinked fragments do better than the [[tags/article-ish|well-established blog format]]?

How to make use of its non-linearity?

How far could you push it? How much can you leave that article format behind to arrive at something that, perhaps, consists more of links than of endpoints?

Experiments to try‽:
- Could a network of (logical) premises associatively generate a conclusion? Would this conclusion exist within the links? Or within the mind of the explorer? What associations, conclusions would come about?
- What about fragments of stories, places, characters? Would narratives emerge? And change, depending on one's pathway through the nodes? What would the notes have to be like?
- What about collaboration? The idea of maintaining a vault together–maybe even open to / with the public–seems a bit like having a nonlinear, multidimensional conversation
- Could all of this exist within the same vault? What would be the relationship of a node (a cluster) to the whole network?

And then there is that one niche in my private Obsidian vault that tackles a bunch of these questions, somewhere between network theory, information systems and aesthetics. It would be interesting to dive back into those and explore them again here, but is *that* really where we are going? lol
