Epaa bom dia! Tudo bem?

I have decided to finally write up and share some of the things I have encountered in this wonderful country over the course of the last months living in São Paulo, Brazil. A life abroad is a messy collage of impressions and learnings and feelings, so [[Digital Gardens|what better way to share them than through inter-linked fragments]]? 

Roughly, this website follows the ideas of a [[Digital Gardens|digital garden]]. The idea is not to present you with a single coherent blog entry that tells you everything you need to know in a thoroughly manicured manner. Instead, this garden of notes is composed of many more-or-less organic entities that all grow at different stages and speeds, some more mature and some mere seedlings that may or may not evolve into bigger flora. And who knows, maybe the trees in this garden will soon branch out of Brazil and reach other territories... For now, I invite you to take a leisurely stroll around the premises. Feel free to point out anything you notice to the gardener!
